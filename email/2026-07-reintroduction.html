Hey —

It’s been a long time. Years, actually. You signed up to hear from me and then I went quiet — so let me fix that, because a lot happened in the silence.

The big one: I split my books in two.

My science fiction still lives under my own name. But the thrillers — the dark stuff, the stories that follow you down the hall after you close them — now live under a pen name: Daniel Scott.

The name isn’t random. Daniel Scott is my late brother — his first and middle name. Putting his name on these books felt like the right way to keep him on the page with me. Every thriller I write now carries it.

And I haven’t been idle under that name. I just released a new novel: A Friend in Debt — Book 4 of a series called Dark Pursuits. It’s dark, it’s fast, and it’s about the kind of debts that don’t die with the people who owed them.
